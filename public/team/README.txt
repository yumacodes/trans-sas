Team photos go here.

1. Save each photo as JPG or WebP, portrait, at least 800 x 1000 px (4:5), face in the upper third.
2. Name it after the person, e.g. ama-mensah.jpg.
3. In src/data/team.ts set that person's `photo` to '/team/ama-mensah.jpg'.

Until a photo is set, the card shows the person's initials.
